Curator & Concept

Valerie Starovoit

"Art is not the representation of form, but rather the suspension of space between gravity and flight."

Based in Dublin, Valerie Starovoit is a 45-year-old contemporary fine artist whose practice reconciles raw materials with ethereal weights. Her works bridge clay, bronze, sand, and gold leaf, creating structural spaces that feel wind-swept and light.

Valerie recently graduated from Liberties College in Dublin with top honors, obtaining multiple distinctions in Sculpture, Ceramics, and Painting. She has now been admitted to the prestigious National College of Art and Design (NCAD) to continue her academic research and production.

Honours & Credentials

  • Winner of the SCC Art, Craft & Design Award 2026

    Awarded for her outstanding granite-clay-bronze sculpture "The Rape of Europe".

  • Liberties College Dublin Graduate

    Achieved top marks with consecutive distinctions across Painting and Fine Ceramics.

  • NCAD Admissions 2026

    Admitted to the National College of Art and Design for advanced Contemporary Fine Art.
