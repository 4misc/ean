Curator & Concept

Valeriia Starovoit

"I strive to translate the massive weight of sculpture and Dublin granite into a state of visual flight, letting materials release their gravity."

Valeriia Starovoit is a 45-year-old contemporary fine artist based in Dublin. Her work is a dialogue between raw, geological mass and fluid, atmospheric weights. By pairing materials like coarse sandstone, clay, and granite with polished steel, bronze, and canvas, she maps structural spaces that feel breathable, organic, and alive.

She recently graduated from Liberties College in Dublin with top honours, achieving multiple distinctions in Sculpture, Ceramics, and Painting. Valerie has now been admitted to the prestigious National College of Art and Design (NCAD) to expand her research in fine art.

Honours & Credentials

  • Winner of the SCC Art, Craft & Design Award 2026

    Awarded for her outstanding sculpture "The Rape of Europe", merging bronze, clay, and Dublin granite.

  • Liberties College Dublin Graduate

  • NCAD Admissions 2026

    Admitted to the National College of Art and Design to pursue advanced Fine Art research.
